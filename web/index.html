Fewer pushed queues, smarter buses.

Students push and cut the queue at campus bus stops because buses overflow at class changes and run half-empty in between. BusWatch predicts where crowds form from the class timetable and shows how to move buses to meet them.

How it works

Timetable

Students board at LRT and the residences before class starts, then queue at APU when it ends at hh:50.

Predict

A machine-learning model forecasts the queue at every stop for every half-hour departure.

Find gaps

Compare predicted queues with seats offered to see where buses overflow.

Simulate & recommend

Test schedule changes and get plain-language fixes with their expected impact.
